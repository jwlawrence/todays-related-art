# Home (/) — surface brief

**Scope & mode:** Operate. The app's only high-frequency surface; answers "which related art does my child have?" at four distances (morning, night-before, weekly, glance).

**Audience & job:** HCPS parent, one-handed on a phone, seconds of attention. Job: read the class, pack the bag. Weekly planning is secondary, served on the same surface.

**Task & content:** Today's resolved class per student (the leaf on the board), the bring-note, the Mon–Fri week as the contents index, day switching via fore-edge tab rail or index rows. When today is off, the next school day's board shows with a "no school today" slip — the answer stays primary.

**Chosen direction & memorable moment:** Manual Acetate Tab Board world (see DESIGN.md). The memorable moment: the day's full-strength section board owning the viewport, and a tapped tab hinging its board in with the 90ms two-frame step — the selected tab visually merges with its board (the tab IS the board).

**Constraints:** Color never alone (every board/tab/swatch carries its name in text). Vermilion strictly for errata (unmapped color, load failure). Anonymous-first; sign-in is a header affordance, primary only on the empty-state title page. No district affiliation implied (colophon says "not affiliated").

**Unresolved:** Whether the week index should extend to next week when the current week is mostly past (Fri/weekend); tab-rail letters are M/T/W/T/F initials — ambiguity accepted because the index disambiguates.
